AutoColor

Automatic colour correction for your photos. Add photos and get corrected copies back with no sliders. Everything runs on your device.

Add photos

Drop photos here or choose them. JPEG, PNG and WebP work everywhere. HEIC works where your browser can open it.

Google Drive input will be available once it is set up.

Your photos

    How it works

    AutoColor measures each photo (brightness, the darkest and brightest tones, colour cast, colourfulness and noise) and only changes what measures out of range. It then sets exposure, white balance, shadows, highlights, black and white points, contrast, vibrance and noise reduction and renders the photo in linear light with the LightCraft develop pipeline compiled to WebAssembly. Photos that already look balanced are changed only a little.

    The corrected photo keeps its full size and is saved as a high quality JPEG named after the original with -autocolor added. Camera RAW files are not supported. Photo metadata such as camera details and location is not copied to the new file. Results are automatic and will not suit every photo, so keep your originals.

    Privacy and credits

    Your photos are processed on your device and are never uploaded. This page sets no cookies and has no analytics.

    If you use Google Drive, AutoColor reads only the files you pick (with the drive.file permission) and stores nothing. AutoColor's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
